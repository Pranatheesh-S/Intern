2.1 Diversity in Plants and Animals Around Us

Activity 2.1 — Let us explore and record

With your teacher, arrange a walk out to a nearby park or patch of forest.
As you go, look closely at the many plants, insects, birds and animals around you — and notice the day's weather, whether warm, chilly or breezy.
Gather a few leaves and flowers that have already fallen, and build them into a scrapbook.
Treat every plant and animal gently: disturb nothing, and pick no leaves or flowers.
Jot down what you notice — the stem, the leaves, the flowers, anything striking — in Table 2.1.

📝Quick practice

Q1. Which plant has a soft stem?
Grass has a soft, thin stem that bends easily; the others have hard stems.
Q2. What should you NOT do during a nature walk?
We observe and record without harming — never pluck leaves or flowers.
Q3. Why do we observe plants carefully?
Careful observation lets us record real features and compare plants — the heart of this activity.
