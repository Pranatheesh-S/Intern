The grid & hemispheres

Latitude and longitude together are a place's two coordinates. With them you can locate anywhere — for example, Delhi lies at ~29°N, 77°E.

All the parallels and meridians together form a grid (grid lines) over the globe.

The Prime Meridian splits Earth into the Western and Eastern hemispheres; the Equator splits it into the Northern and Southern hemispheres.
