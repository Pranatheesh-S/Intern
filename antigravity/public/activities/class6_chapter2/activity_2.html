Activity 2.2 — Let us appreciate

Shut your eyes for half a minute and bring to mind one plant and one animal you have watched closely and truly admired.
Now each of you draws that plant and that animal on the class blackboard.
Look at everything drawn — what do you notice about all these different plants and animals?

✎ What our class drew
How many different kinds did the whole class draw in all? And do you think many more kinds exist beyond those on the board?
The full variety of plants and animals living in one area makes up the biodiversity of that region.
Every living thing in that variety has its own role. Trees, for instance, give birds and other animals food and shelter, while those animals carry seeds to new places after eating fruit.
Can you think of more such give-and-take? It shows how plants and animals rely on one another.

📝Quick practice

Q1. The whole variety of plants and animals in a region is called its…
Biodiversity is the full variety of living things found in a place.
Q2. Trees shelter birds; birds spread tree seeds. This shows living things are…
Each does a job the other needs — that mutual reliance is interdependence.
Q3. When the whole class draws different plants and animals, the board mainly shows us…
Many different drawings reveal the variety of life — the biodiversity — around us.
